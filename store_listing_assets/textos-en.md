## 3) Description
```
Maity helps you communicate with more clarity, persuasion, and empathy by turning your real, everyday conversations into feedback you can actually apply.

Maity analyzes how you actually communicate day to day: it shows you your clarity, structure, persuasion, empathy, and adaptability, along with practical signals like filler words and how balanced your speaking time was. Instead of generic advice, you get specific coaching based on conversations you actually had.

Chat with Maity, your always-available communication and productivity coach. Prepare for a difficult meeting, brainstorm what to say, turn loose notes into a presentation ready to deliver, or drop in a document (PDF, Word, or Excel) and let Maity read and summarize it for you. Connect your calendar and Maity gets ahead of your day with a proactive daily briefing, and it can generate clear meeting minutes directly from your conversations.

Whether you're preparing for an important conversation, looking to grow at work, or leading a team, Maity gives you honest feedback and a coach in your corner, every day.

Maity is free to download and use. The free plan has daily and monthly usage limits on features such as conversation analysis and Maity Chat. The Maity Pro subscription removes those limits and is purchased outside the app, on our website; Maity does not make purchases through the Microsoft Store commerce system.
```
